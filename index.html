I am an AI researcher focused on deep learning model efficiency, neural morphogenesis, and representation learning. My research centers around developing extreme ultra-low-bit model quantization (such as sub-4-bit and data-free ternary PTQ) preserving weight geometry, uncovering emergent computational structures via Neural Cellular Automata (c-NCAs), and improving distributional semantics with attentive pooling.

S-DPQ: Block-Level Angular and Norm Matching for Data-Free Ternary Quantization

Ali Sakour
NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Efficient Deep Learning (AXIOM)

Introduced Spatial-Directional Preserving Quantization (S-DPQ), a data-free ternary quantization scheme that aligns weight block geometry through joint cosine similarity and Frobenius norm matching, reinforced with salient channel protection for transformer models.
